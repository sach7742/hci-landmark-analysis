Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) & Motion Analytics

A web-based suite for analyzing human motor performance via Fitts's Law models and computer-vision landmark tracking powered by Google MediaPipe.

Fitts's Law Motor Benchmark

Evaluates target acquisition throughput ($MT = a + b \cdot ID$) across varying distances ($D$) and target widths ($W$) for pointing devices.

MediaPipe Kinematic Tracker

Real-time pose, face, and hand mesh extraction using browser-based neural network models with high-frequency time-series CSV logging.
